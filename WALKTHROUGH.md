## Cold-email framing (the spine)

- **Proof:** a resolution layer ON their orchestration platform — evidence trails, human-gated regulatory steps, and a measured evaluation harness.
- **The number:** ~230 ops hours and ₹4.2L avoided per 100 exceptions/month (simulated model).
- **Close:** 20 minutes with you or Vineet — or just open the link; the hero case runs end to end.

## Recording tips

- **Resolution:** 1920×1080, browser zoom 100–110%, hide bookmarks bar.
- **Cursor:** deliberate moves; pause half a beat before each scroll so cuts are clean.
- **Trim dead time:** the Approval Flow animation is the only place real-time pacing helps — keep it.
- **GIF alternative:** shots 4→7 only (~40s), if the email needs an inline visual; link the Loom for the full pass.
- **First frame of shot 1** doubles as the email thumbnail — the "Today vs Proposed" flow is the whole pitch in one image.
- Say the simulated-data disclaimer once (shot 3 or 4), not repeatedly.
- Don't mention hexalog.com — only hexalog.in matters here.
